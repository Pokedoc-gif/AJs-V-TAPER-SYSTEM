Workout Pages

Monday — Upper A

Focus: Lats (Width) + Side Delts + Rear Delts

  • Weighted Wide-grip Pull-ups — 3 x 6–8
  • Chest-supported Machine Row — 3 x 10–12
  • Heavy Lateral Raises (DB) — 4 x 8–10
  • Face Pulls — 3 x 15–20
  • Dumbbell Pullovers — 3 x 12–15
  • Straight-arm Pulldowns — 2 x 15–20

Exercise Instructions

Weighted Wide-grip Pull-ups

Grip bar wider than shoulders. Hang fully at the bottom. Pull elbows down and back. Squeeze lats at the top. No kipping or swinging.

Chest-supported Machine Row

Chest flat against the pad. Feet planted. Pull handles toward lower chest. Squeeze shoulder blades together for 1 second.

Heavy Lateral Raises (DB)

Stand with a slight forward lean. Raise dumbbells to shoulder height. Control the negative.

Face Pulls

Rope at face height. Pull toward your face, then pull the rope apart at the end for rear delts.

Dumbbell Pullovers

Lie across bench with shoulders supported and hips low. Lower the DB behind your head for a deep lat stretch.

Straight-arm Pulldowns

Use a rope attachment. Keep arms straight. Push down and squeeze lats at the bottom.

Sciatica reminder: Do 5 nerve glides per leg between every set.

Tuesday — Upper B

Focus: Mid-Back Thickness + Biceps + Volume Laterals

  • Seated Cable Rows (V-grip) — 3 x 8–10
  • Close-grip Pulldowns — 3 x 10–12
  • Lateral Raises (Volume) — 4 x 15–20
  • Rear Delt Machine Flyes — 3 x 15–20
  • Incline Dumbbell Press — 3 x 12–15
  • Hammer Curls — 3 x 10–12

Exercise Instructions

Seated Cable Rows (V-grip)

Use chest pad if available. Squeeze shoulder blades together and hold the contraction for 1 second.

Close-grip Pulldowns

Hands shoulder-width apart. Pull to upper chest and keep elbows tucked.

Lateral Raises (Volume)

Use lighter weight and focus on the burn and mind-muscle connection.

Rear Delt Machine Flyes

Chest against pad. Open arms wide and squeeze rear delts at full extension.

Incline Dumbbell Press

Light weight for shoulder health. Feet flat on the floor. No arch.

Hammer Curls

Neutral grip with elbows locked at sides. Controlled reps to build bicep peak.

Sciatica reminder: Do 5 nerve glides per leg between every set.

Wednesday — Upper C

Focus: High-rep Lat Pump + Shoulder Health

  • Lat Pulldowns (Wide, High Reps) — 3 x 15–20
  • Dumbbell Pullovers (Slow Tempo) — 3 x 15–20
  • Lateral Raises (Moderate) — 3 x 12–15
  • Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press — 3 x 10–12
  • Cable Cross-overs — 3 x 15–20
  • Face Pulls — 2 x 20

Exercise Instructions

Lat Pulldowns (Wide, High Reps)

Use light weight. Get a full stretch at the top and full squeeze at the bottom.

Dumbbell Pullovers (Slow Tempo)

Use a 3-second negative on the way down. Focus on the deep lat stretch.

Lateral Raises (Moderate)

Use medium weight and controlled tempo. No swinging.

Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Back firmly against support. Press up and control the negative.

Cable Cross-overs

Light weight for a chest pump. Keep your back neutral with no arch.

Face Pulls

Burn out rear delts with controlled reps and full range of motion.

Sciatica reminder: Do 5 nerve glides per leg between every set.

Thursday — Legs + Core

Focus: Leg Health + Core Stability

  • Leg Press (Feet High & Wide) — 4 x 12–15
  • Lying Leg Curls — 4 x 12–15
  • Leg Extensions — 3 x 15–20
  • Glute Bridges (Bodyweight) — 3 x 25
  • Seated Calf Raises — 4 x 15–20
  • Plank — 3 x 60s
  • Side Plank — 3 x 45s per side

Exercise Instructions

Leg Press (Feet High & Wide)

Back fully braced against the pad. Controlled tempo. Do not lock out knees.

Lying Leg Curls

Hamstring focus. Control the negative on the way down.

Leg Extensions

Isolated quads. Use light weight and squeeze at the top.

Glute Bridges (Bodyweight)

Push through heels and squeeze glutes at the top for 2 seconds.

Seated Calf Raises

Use full stretch at the bottom and full squeeze at the top.

Plank

Knees down if needed. Keep a neutral spine and squeeze glutes and core.

Side Plank

Keep hips stacked and avoid sagging.

Sciatica reminder: This is your only leg day. Keep weights moderate—focus on form, not ego.

Friday — Arms & Shoulders

Focus: Side Delts + Biceps + Triceps

  • Lateral Raises (Dropset) — 4 x 10+10+10
  • Seated Arnold Press — 3 x 10–12
  • EZ-Bar Skull Crushers — 3 x 12–15
  • Preacher Curls — 3 x 10–12
  • Tricep Pushdowns (Rope) — 3 x 12–15
  • Dumbbell Curls (Strict) — 3 x 10–12
  • Cable Lateral Raises — 2 x 20

Exercise Instructions

Lateral Raises (Dropset)

Start heavy, drop weight twice, and do not rest between drops.

Seated Arnold Press

Back supported. Rotate palms outward at the top of the press.

EZ-Bar Skull Crushers

Lie flat on the bench with no arch. Lower bar to forehead and keep elbows pointed up.

Preacher Curls

Elbows supported on the pad. Keep the movement strict with no swinging.

Tricep Pushdowns (Rope)

Keep elbows locked at sides and spread the rope at the bottom.

Dumbbell Curls (Strict)

No swinging. Control the negative on every rep.

Cable Lateral Raises

Use light weight and high reps to completely burn out the side delts.

Sciatica reminder: Lighter day overall—your back gets a break.

Morning Abs Routine

Do this immediately upon waking, before breakfast, every day

  • Dead Bug — 10 per side
  • Plank — 60 seconds
  • Side Plank — 45 seconds per side
  • Bird-Dog — 12 per side
  • Hollow Body Hold — 45 seconds

Progression: Add 5 seconds to each hold every week.

Bedtime Sciatica Reset

8-minute routine • Right before bed every night

  • Phase 1: Nerve Glides — 15 reps per leg
  • Phase 2: Figure-4 Stretch — 90 seconds per side
  • Phase 3: Supine Hamstring Stretch — 60 seconds per side
  • Phase 4: Final Nerve Glides — 10 slow reps per leg

Sleeping rules: side sleep with pillow between knees, no stomach sleeping, no intense stretching before bed.
