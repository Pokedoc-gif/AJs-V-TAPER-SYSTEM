Daily Workouts

Monday: Upper A — Heavy Width + Chest Starter

Focus: Lats, Upper Chest, Side Delts

Workout Calories: 450

  • Heavy Lateral Raises (DB) — 4 x 8–10 — 45s
  • Face Pulls — 3 x 15–20 — 45s
  • Dumbbell Pullovers — 3 x 12–15 — 45s

Focus: All heads / Lower Chest focus

Workout Calories: 480

Sunday: Complete Arms Day

Focus: Biceps, Triceps, Brachialis

Workout Calories: 420

Post Workout Abs Calories: 80

Total Calories Burned: 500

Cardio: 20 min incline walk

Superset Workout

  • Superset 1: Seated Dumbbell Curls — 4 x 8–10 — Biceps (Mass)
  • Superset 1: EZ-Bar Skull Crushers — 4 x 10–12 — Triceps (Mass)
  • Superset 2: Incline Dumbbell Curls — 4 x 12–15 — Biceps (Long Head)
  • Superset 2: Overhead Tricep Extension — 4 x 12–15 — Triceps (Long Head)
  • Superset 3: Preacher Curls — 4 x 10–12 — Biceps (Peak)
  • Superset 3: Tricep Pushdowns (Rope) — 4 x 12–15 — Triceps (Horseshoe)
  • Superset 4: Hammer Curls — 4 x 12–15 — Brachialis
  • Superset 4: Close-Grip Bench Press (DB) — 4 x 10–12 — Triceps (Mass)
  • Finisher: Cable Curls — 3 x 20–25 — Blood flow
  • Finisher: Cable Tricep Kickbacks — 3 x 20–25 — Blood flow

Complete each superset back-to-back, then rest. Stop if you experience pain or nerve symptoms.

Post Workout Abs Routine

Do this immediately after your daily workout, every day

2 rounds • minimal rest between exercises • 1–2 min rest between rounds

  • Combined Dead Bug — 20 reps
  • Crunches — 10 reps
  • Flutter Kicks — 20 per side
  • Russian Twist — 50 per side

Add 5 seconds to each hold every week.

Bedtime Sciatica Reset

8-minute routine • Do this right before bed every single night

  • Nerve Glides — 15 reps per leg
  • Figure-4 Stretch — 90 seconds per side
  • Supine Hamstring Stretch — 60 seconds per side
  • Final Nerve Glides — 10 slow reps per leg

Sleep on your side with a pillow between your knees. Never sleep on your stomach.

Complete Exercise Instruction Library

Pull-Ups

Grip wider than shoulders. Hang fully, pull elbows down and back, no swinging.

Incline Dumbbell Press

Bench at 30°, lower to upper chest, and press with control.

Chest-Supported Machine Row

Keep your chest against the pad and pull the handles toward your lower chest.

Lateral Raises

Use a slight forward lean and raise to shoulder height without swinging.

Face Pulls

Pull the rope toward your face and separate the ends at the finish.

Dumbbell Pullovers

Keep your shoulders supported and control the deep lat stretch.

Straight-Arm Pulldowns

Keep the arms straight, torso still, and squeeze the lats at the bottom.

Seated Cable Rows

Pull toward the lower chest and squeeze the shoulder blades together.

Close-Grip Pulldowns

Pull toward the upper chest while keeping the elbows tucked.

Flat Dumbbell Bench Press

Lower the dumbbells to chest level and press without excessive arching.

Rear Delt Flyes

Keep your chest supported and open the arms with control.

Incline Dumbbell Press (light)

Use a light load, controlled reps, and focus on the upper-chest pump.

Hammer Curls

Use a neutral grip, keep the elbows at your sides, and control the negative.

Lat Pulldowns

Use a full stretch and slow, controlled repetitions.

Cable Cross-Overs

Pull the hands across the body and squeeze the chest.

Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Keep the back supported and press straight up with control.

Leg Press

Place the feet high and wide while keeping the back braced against the pad.

Lying Leg Curls

Curl toward the glutes slowly and avoid lifting the hips.

Leg Extensions

Extend the legs and squeeze the quads at the top.

Glute Bridges

Drive through the feet and squeeze the glutes at the top.

Seated Calf Raises

Lower the heels for a full stretch and press up high.

Plank

Keep the body straight with the glutes and core tight.

Side Plank

Stack the hips and maintain a neutral spine without sagging.

Arnold Press

Rotate the palms outward as you press, using a controlled tempo.

EZ-Bar Skull Crushers

Lower the bar toward the forehead while keeping the elbows pointed upward.

Preacher Curls

Keep the elbows supported on the pad and perform strict curls.

Tricep Pushdowns (Rope)

Keep the elbows at your sides, push down, and spread the rope ends.

Dumbbell Curls

Avoid swinging and control the lowering phase for 2–3 seconds.

High-to-Low Cable Cross-Overs

Set the pulleys high, pull down and across, and squeeze the lower chest.

Dips

Lean slightly forward to emphasize the lower chest and triceps.

Decline Dumbbell Press

Use a slight decline, pause at the bottom, and press with control.

Push-Ups

Keep the body straight and use a full range of motion.

Seated Dumbbell Curls

Sit upright, keep the elbows still, curl through the full range, and control the lowering phase.

Incline Dumbbell Curls

Set the bench to an incline, let the arms hang naturally, and curl without moving the shoulders.

Overhead Tricep Extension

Keep the elbows pointed forward, lower the weight behind the head, and extend without flaring the elbows.

Close-Grip Bench Press (Dumbbell)

Keep the dumbbells close together over the chest and press while keeping the elbows controlled.

Cable Curls

Stand tall, keep the elbows fixed, curl toward the shoulders, and squeeze the biceps.

Cable Tricep Kickbacks

Hinge slightly forward, keep the upper arm still, and extend the elbow until the triceps contract.
