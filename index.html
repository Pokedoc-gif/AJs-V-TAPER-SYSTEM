Morning Abs Routine

Do this immediately after your morning walk, before breakfast, every day

3 rounds • minimal rest between exercises • 2 min rest between rounds

  • Dead Bug — 10 per side
  • Plank — 60 seconds
  • Side Plank — 45 seconds per side
  • Bird-Dog — 12 per side
  • Hollow Body Hold — 45 seconds

Add 5 seconds to each hold every week.

Bedtime Sciatica Reset

8-minute routine • Do this right before bed every single night

  • Nerve Glides — 15 reps per leg
  • Figure-4 Stretch — 90 seconds per side
  • Supine Hamstring Stretch — 60 seconds per side
  • Final Nerve Glides — 10 slow reps per leg

Sleep on your side with a pillow between your knees. Never sleep on your stomach.

Complete Exercise Instruction Library

Pull-Ups

Grip wider than shoulders. Hang fully, pull elbows down and back, no swinging.

Chest-Supported Machine Row

Chest flat against pad. Pull handles to lower chest.

Lateral Raises

Slight forward lean. Raise to shoulder height. No swinging.

Face Pulls

Pull rope toward face and apart at the end.

Dumbbell Pullovers

Shoulders supported, hips low, deep lat stretch.

Straight-Arm Pulldowns

Arms straight, torso still, squeeze lats at bottom.

Seated Cable Rows

Pull to lower chest and squeeze shoulder blades together.

Close-Grip Pulldowns

Pull bar to upper chest with elbows tucked.

Rear Delt Flyes

Chest against pad, open arms wide, squeeze rear delts.

Incline Dumbbell Press

Bench at 30–45°, feet flat, no arch.

Hammer Curls

Neutral grip, elbows at sides, controlled negative.

Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Back supported, press straight up with control.

Cable Cross-Overs

Pull hands down and across the body, no arch.

Leg Press

Feet high and wide, back fully braced against pad.

Lying Leg Curls

Lie face down and curl toward glutes with control.

Leg Extensions

Seated on machine, extend legs and squeeze quads.

Glute Bridges

Lie on back, lift hips, squeeze glutes at top.

Seated Calf Raises

Lower heels for a full stretch and press up high.

Dead Bug

Lower back flat, extend opposite arm and leg slowly.

Plank

Forearms down, body straight, glutes and core tight.

Side Plank

Hips stacked, neutral spine, no sagging.

Bird-Dog

Extend opposite arm and leg, hold for 3 seconds.

Hollow Body Hold

Lower back pinned to floor, hold only what you control.

Arnold Press

Rotate palms outward at the top, controlled tempo.

EZ-Bar Skull Crushers

Lower to forehead with elbows pointed up.

Preacher Curls

Elbows supported on the pad, strict curl.

Tricep Pushdowns

Elbows at sides, push rope down and spread ends.

Dumbbell Curls

No swinging, control the negative for 2–3 seconds.

Cable Lateral Raises

Use light weight, braced core, raise to shoulder height.
